Tuan-Minh Nguyen

Random walks in random environments; superdiffusivity

arXiv:2608.21701 · PDF

Research summary

We establish that in dimension three the diffusion coefficient grows, in the Tauberian sense, as the square root of the logarithm of time, up to iterated-logarithmic corrections.

[15] 2026 · Preprint

Limit theorems for random walks with spatio-temporal drift

Ngo P. N. Ngoc, Tuan-Minh Nguyen

Random walks with drift; limit theorems

arXiv:2605.17725 · PDF

Research summary

We establish phase transitions for walks with time-dependent and position-dependent drift. The limiting behavior includes Gaussian and non-Gaussian limits and almost sure localization on a hypersphere.

[14] 2026 · Preprint

"True" self-avoiding walks on general trees

Tuan-Minh Nguyen

Self-avoiding walks; trees

arXiv:2604.24389 · PDF

Research summary

For exponentially decreasing edge weights, we prove transience when the branching-ruin number exceeds 1/2 and recurrence when it is below 1/2, resolving a question of Kosygina.

[13] 2026 · Preprint

Once-excited random walks on general trees

Duy-Bao Le, Tuan-Minh Nguyen

Excited random walks; trees

arXiv:2602.16934 · PDF

Research summary

We establish a recurrence–transience phase transition for once-excited walks on trees with polynomial growth and independent random biases, in terms of the branching-ruin number.

[12] 2024 · Preprint

Strongly vertex-reinforced jump process on graphs with bounded degree

Andrea Collevecchio, Tuan-Minh Nguyen

Reinforced processes; localization

arXiv:2401.04366 · PDF

Research summary

We prove finiteness of the visited set for nondecreasing reinforcement with integrable reciprocal. For reinforcement comparable to a superlinear polynomial, the process eventually remains on a star with a unique vertex of unbounded local time.

[11] 2024 (preprint year) · Forthcoming

Finding pure Nash equilibria in large random games

Andrea Collevecchio, Tuan-Minh Nguyen, Ziwen Zhong

Mathematics of Operations Research (to appear)

Random games; random walks on hypercubes

arXiv:2406.09732 · PDF

Research summary

We study random games with two actions per player and independent payoffs, allowing ties. Best response dynamics and other nearest-neighbor walks reach a pure Nash equilibrium before hitting a trap with high probability.

[10] 2022 · Published

Vertex-reinforced jump process on the integers with nonlinear reinforcement

Andrea Collevecchio, Tuan-Minh Nguyen, Stanislav Volkov

We establish recurrence for reinforcement with nonintegrable reciprocal, and localization on three vertices under an additional regularity condition in the integrable case. Only one vertex then has unbounded local time.
